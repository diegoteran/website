How to Play With the Cards

1. Swap (Optional): Click any 2 Action Cards in the left column to swap their order.

2. Build Overlapping Stack: Click the Top or Bottom half directly on each card to rotate that action to the top and tuck it into the stack. Cards #1–#4 execute only their visible top half; the bottom card executes both halves!

3. Play Stack: The Purple Star Token (⭐) steps down your stack. Spend 🔥 Fuel at a Telescope to flip & observe Space Cards, or at a Hook to catch observed entities.

4. Upgrade: At the end of each round, click any Level 1 Action Card, flip it in 3D to inspect its Level 2 side, and confirm its upgrade.
