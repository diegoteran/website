GalaxSea

Welcome to GalaxSea! Strategically program your actions, align your ship parts, and catch celestial entities in deep space.

Object of the Game: Hook 3 celestial entities of your chosen Target Tier in a single round before Round 6 ends!

Target Tiers

System Tier • Catch 3 Planets

Setup

  1. Choose Target Tier: Pick your challenge tier from Page 1 (3 Moons recommended for your first game).
  2. Ship Hexagon: Shuffle the 6 Ship Cards (3 Telescopes, 3 Hooks) face-down in a center hexagon.
  3. Direction Pointer: Place in center pointing North. Flip North Ship Card face-up.
  4. Space Hexagon: Shuffle the 6 Space Cards face-down in an outer ring matching Ship Cards.
  5. Action Hand: Take the 5 Action Cards (all start at Level 1).

CARD 2 — FACE A (BASE)

Round Overview

The game is played over up to 6 rounds. Each round proceeds through the following steps:

Step 1: Shuffle & Place in Column

Shuffle all 5 Action Cards and place them face-up in a vertical column (for Round 1, all cards start at Level 1).

Step 2: Optional Swap

You may swap the positions of any 2 cards in your column.

Step 3: Program Actions

Each Action Card has two action halves. For each of the top 4 cards, choose one action by rotating the card so the chosen action faces up (the unused half is tucked and covered by the card below it).

The bottom (5th) card grants both printed actions in the order you set it (top action first, then bottom action).

PAGE 3
CARD 2 — FACE B (BASE)

Round Overview (cont.)

Step 4: Execute Programmed Actions

Execute your 6 programmed actions strictly from top to bottom. Actions are: Rotate (p. 5), Swap (p. 6), and Spend Fuel (p. 7–8).

Caught Rewards (Optional Anytime): At any time while executing actions, you may also activate bonus actions from newly caught entities (p. 8–9).

Step 5: Release Catches

Return all caught Space Cards to their outer positions. Each stays observed, pointing inward toward the ship. (Catches reset every round—hook all 3 in a single round to win!)

Step 6: Level Up

At round end, flip 1 Action Card to Level 2. If all 5 cards are already Level 2 and you have not achieved victory, the game ends in defeat.

Action: Rotate

The Direction Card in the center indicates your ship's active section. Each Rotate action turns the pointer 1 position around the hexagon:

  • Clockwise: Turn pointer 1 position clockwise.
  • Counter-Clockwise: Turn pointer 1 position counter-clockwise.
  • Any Direction: Choose to turn pointer 1 position either clockwise or counter-clockwise.
Immediate Ship Reveal

Whenever the Direction Pointer turns to face a face-down Ship Card, immediately flip that Ship Card face-up so its ship part (Telescope or Hook) is revealed!

Action: Swap

Exchange any two adjacent Ship Cards (face-up or face-down). The pointer's direction does not limit which cards you swap.

Immediate Ship Reveal

If the pointer faces a face-down Ship Card after a swap, flip it face-up immediately.

Action: Spend Fuel

1 Fuel = 1 Power on the aimed facility: Telescope to Observe (p. 7) or Hook to Hook (p. 8).

Combining Fuel (Chaining)

Adjacent Fuel actions in your column can combine into a single shared pool for extra power!

Spend Fuel: Observing

When the Direction Pointer aims at a Telescope, spend Fuel to observe the Space Card directly opposite:

1. Reveal Face-Up

If the Space Card is face-down, flip it face-up. Once revealed, Space Cards remain face-up for the rest of the game!

2. Sensor Power: 1 Fuel = 1 Telescope

Each 1 Fuel spent generates 1 Telescope Power . Compare your total power to the entity costs printed on the card.

3. Rotate to Observe

Rotate the card so an entity you can afford faces inward toward your ship. If your power meets both costs, you choose which one to observe!

PAGE 7
CARD 4 — FACE B (BASE)

Spend Fuel: Capturing

When the Direction Pointer aims at a Hook, spend Fuel to capture an observed celestial entity:

1. Target Must Be Observed

Hooks cannot catch face-down or sideways entities. The entity you want to catch must already be observed (facing inward toward your ship).

2. Reeling Power: 1 Fuel = 1 Hook

Each 1 Fuel spent generates 1 Hook Power . If your power meets or exceeds the entity's Hook cost, you catch it!

3. Pull & Anchor Hook

Slide the Space Card over your Hook. That Hook is now Anchored (cannot be swapped) until released, and its caught reward unlocks immediately!

Caught Rewards Guide

Every caught Space Card features a powerful bonus action along its outer banner:

Activate Anytime During Your Turn

You may trigger a caught entity's reward at any time while executing your programmed actions—even between Action Cards!

Combining Reward Fuel

If a reward grants Fuel, you can combine it directly with Fuel from your Action Cards to power high-cost Telescopes or Hooks!

Tucking Card After Use

Once activated, slide the Space Card slightly under your Hook to indicate its reward has been spent for the current round.

One Reward Per Round

Each catch provides its bonus reward once per round. When catches are released at round end, their rewards reset.

Action & Icon Glossary

Fuel: Energy to power active Telescope or Hook.

Telescope: Sensor facility. Reveals and observes Space Cards.

Hook: Fishing arm. Captures observed celestial entities.

Rotate: Turn Direction Pointer 1 space (CW, CCW, or Any).

Swap: Exchange 2 adjacent Ship Cards (unless anchored).

Slash (/) and Plus (+) Symbols

Slash (/): Choose exactly ONE of the two printed actions.
Plus (+): Execute BOTH linked actions in either order.

Round Sequence Summary
  1. Shuffle: Place 5 Action Cards in a vertical column.
  2. Swap: Optional swap of any 2 cards in your column.
  3. Program: Top 4 cards pick one action; 5th card gets both!
  4. Execute: Top to bottom. Use caught rewards anytime.
  5. Release Catches: Return catches (cards stay observed).
  6. Level Up: Flip 1 Action Card to its Level 2 face.

Deep Space Observing & The Parallel Rule

Supernovas and Black Holes are massive cosmic forces located far out in deep space, requiring higher sensor output (3–4 Telescopes) to observe.

During setup, Space Cards start lying parallel (sideways) to the ship's edge.

Parallel = Unobserved: If you spend Fuel on a face-down Deep Space card but do not meet either entity's Telescope cost, flip it face-up but leave it sideways. The card is revealed, but neither entity is locked in yet! It cannot be hooked until a future Telescope action generates enough power to rotate it inward.

Rules Clarifications

1. Skipping Impossible Actions

If an action cannot be performed (e.g. no adjacent card to swap, or hook aiming at nothing), skip it with no penalty.

2. Deciding Fuel in Advance

Decide Fuel spent before flipping a face-down Space Card. If your Fuel falls short of both costs, it remains unobserved.

3. Combining Fuel

Bonus Fuel from caught rewards combines freely with Action Card Fuel to meet higher power costs!

4. Multi-Action Rewards

If a reward grants two actions (with a +), resolve both in either order, one after the other.

5. Mandatory Fuel & Rotation

If 1 Fuel powers a Telescope facing an observed 2-cost entity, you must rotate to observe the 1-cost entity if able.

6. The 3-Hook Limit

Each Hook holds 1 catch. Winning requires holding all 3 of your target entities across all 3 Hooks in that round.

Behind the Scenes

The 18-Card Origin

The 18-Card Limit

Card game manufacturers print cards on press sheets of exactly 18 cards. This constraint inspired the beloved "18-card microgame" challenge: designing a complete, deep tabletop experience using only a single sheet of paper.

The Birth of GalaxSea

GalaxSea was designed around this challenge. To fit an entire modular ship, an outer galaxy ring, and programmable actions into a pocket mint tin, every card had to work double duty.

Double-sided ship cards and dual-ended space cards made it all possible with zero wasted space!
